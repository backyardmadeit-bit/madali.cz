    Long bio

    David Madali is a Prague-based analyst of the defence industry, critical raw materials and the space economy. In 2024 he founded Bohemia Defense, an independent analysis project on Europe's defence industry, security and foreign policy, whose posts reach up to 450,000 views on LinkedIn. In 2026 he built OreOrbit, a free, live atlas of the mines, refineries and sea routes behind the ten minerals Europe's defence depends on, and its monthly Dependency Index. BBC News Chinese has quoted him as an analyst. His background is in economics (University of Economics and Management, Prague), economic diplomacy at the Czech Ministry of Foreign Affairs and export consulting at CzechTrade, where he set up a cluster of 12 security-industry companies.
